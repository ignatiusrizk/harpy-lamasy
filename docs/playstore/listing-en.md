```
LAMASY is an all-in-one management app (ERP) for laundry businesses — built for owners and outlet staff. Run your entire operation from one place: point of sale, orders, finances, inventory, staff, and reports, with AI on your side.

KEY FEATURES

• Point of Sale (POS)
  Create orders fast — multiple services, discounts & down payments. Print receipts and labels to a Bluetooth thermal printer. Add orders by voice with AI assistance.

• Order Management
  Track order status (queue → in process → ready → done) on a Kanban board. Send status updates to customers via WhatsApp.

• Finances
  Record cash in/out, manage B2B receivables, and view financial reports (daily, monthly, profit & loss) aligned with SAK EMKM standards.

• Inventory & Purchasing
  Manage materials, stock movements, and purchase orders.

• Staff & Attendance
  Clock-in with selfie and location verification (geofence), shift scheduling, and automatic payroll.

• Customer Loyalty
  Points program, member tiers, deposit wallet, and referrals.

• Pickup & Delivery
  Assign couriers, set zones, and track pickup/delivery status.

• Reports & Analytics
  A clean dashboard, detailed reports, and data export.

• Multi-Outlet (HQ)
  Owners with several branches can monitor every outlet from one central view.

Great for laundry businesses of any kind — by weight (kiloan), per item, express, or B2B/corporate. Built by PT Harpy Sinergi Mandiri.

An account is required to sign in. Contact us to subscribe.
```
