### M1 — CSRF token eksplisit absen di beberapa endpoint HQ state-changing
- File: `hq/ai-chat.php` (deduct coin), `hq/broadcast.php` (kirim WA + coin), `hq/ai-churning.php` (coin), `hq/layanan.php` (CRUD layanan), `hq/checklist.php`.
- Risiko: CSRF bisa memicu aksi (drain coin / broadcast) tanpa token.
